PM-KISAN: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQS), REGISTRATION, AND GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL
MINISTRY: Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India
SOURCE: Official FAQs and Procedures (https://pmkisan.gov.in)
================================================================================

1. REGISTRATION PROCEDURE
Question: How can a farmer register for the PM-KISAN scheme?
Answer: Farmers can register through multiple convenient channels:
(a) Online Self-Registration: Visit the official portal (https://pmkisan.gov.in) -> Farmers Corner -> Click on "New Farmer Registration". Enter Aadhaar number, mobile number, State, District, Sub-District, Block, Village, land ownership particulars (Survey / Khata / Khasra number, land area in hectares), and bank account details.
(b) Common Service Centres (CSCs): Farmers can visit any nearby village CSC or e-Mitra kiosk where the VLE (Village Level Entrepreneur) assists with online registration upon verification of Aadhaar and land documents.
(c) State Nodal Officers / Revenue Officials: Farmers may submit physical registration forms to the local Village Patwari, Lekhpal, or designated State Agricultural Nodal Officer.

2. MANDATORY DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR REGISTRATION
Question: What documents and details are required to apply for PM-KISAN?
Answer:
1. Aadhaar Card (Mandatory for identification across all States/UTs).
2. Proof of Landholding: Updated Record of Rights (RoR), Jamabandi, or Khatauni issued by the State Revenue Department showing the farmer as the legal owner of cultivable land.
3. Bank Account Details: Savings Bank Account Number and IFSC code of an Aadhaar-linked and NPCI-mapped account.
4. Active Mobile Number for receiving OTP authentications and installment dispatch SMS notifications.

3. BENEFICIARY STATUS TRACKING
Question: How can a farmer check the status of their PM-KISAN application or installment?
Answer: Farmers can check real-time installment status without logging in:
1. Visit https://pmkisan.gov.in.
2. In the "Farmers Corner" section, click on "Know Your Status".
3. Enter your PM-KISAN Registration Number (or Mobile Number) and the security captcha code.
4. The dashboard displays the complete payment timeline, FTO (Fund Transfer Order) generation status, bank account validation status, and reasons for any rejection or pending status.

4. RECTIFICATION AND UPDATES
Question: How can a farmer correct incorrect Aadhaar details or update their bank account details?
Answer: Under "Farmers Corner" on the portal, options are provided for:
- "Name Correction as per Aadhaar": Update legal spelling and details matching UIDAI records.
- "Updation of Self Registered Farmer": Modify land details or personal records.
- Re-seeding bank account with NPCI via your local bank branch if DBT transactions fail.

5. GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL AND HELPLINE CONTACTS
Question: Whom should a farmer contact in case of non-receipt of installments or grievances?
Answer: Multiple institutional grievance channels are maintained by the Government of India:
- PM-KISAN Toll-Free Helpline: 155261 / 1800-115-526
- Direct Ministry Telephone: 011-23381092, 011-24300606
- Email Support: pmkisan-ict@gov.in
- Online Helpdesk Query: Go to PM-KISAN Portal -> "Helpdesk - Query Form". Farmers can log their grievance using Aadhaar or Registration number, receive a tracking ticket, and monitor resolution by the State Agricultural Department.
